Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 武汉云之月电子商务有限公司 (Wuhan Yunyue E-commerce Co., Ltd.) handles information in connection with its internal e-commerce and advertising analytics tools.

1. Information we process

For our own Amazon advertising accounts, our internal tools may process advertising campaign configuration, performance reports, keywords, targets, search terms, spend, attributed sales and related advertising metrics. Authentication credentials and tokens may also be processed solely to maintain authorized API access.

2. Purpose of processing

We use this information for internal reporting, advertising analysis, campaign optimization, account administration, security and troubleshooting. Our tools are not offered or licensed to third-party advertisers.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell Amazon advertising data. Information may be processed by infrastructure, security, analytics or AI service providers only when needed to operate our internal systems, subject to applicable agreements and access controls. API credentials and access tokens are not intentionally included in AI prompts or exposed publicly.

4. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and protected credential storage. Write operations to advertising accounts are designed to be reviewed or controlled by internal authorization rules.

5. Retention

We retain advertising data only for as long as reasonably necessary for internal business, reporting, security or legal purposes, and remove data when it is no longer required where practicable.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact whyzydzsw@163.com.

This website describes an internal company application and does not provide public sign-up or third-party advertising management services.
